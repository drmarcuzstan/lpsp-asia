Why Asia, why now

The world's growth is moving east. Your business should be there.

For European companies, being global is no longer optional. Customers, supply chains, talent and capital are increasingly centred in Asia. Companies that build a real presence in the region gain access to its growth, spread their risk beyond a single home market and learn to compete where the future of many industries is being shaped.

~60%of global economic growth comes from Asia and the Pacific, according to the IMF
~680mpeople live in the ten ASEAN economies of Southeast Asia
2019the EU–Singapore Free Trade Agreement entered into force, removing most tariffs between the two
RCEPthe world's largest trade agreement, in force since 2022, links 15 Asia-Pacific economies

Sources: IMF Regional Economic Outlook for Asia and Pacific; European Commission; ASEAN Secretariat. Figures are rounded.

01

Growth that Europe alone cannot offer

Asia's expanding middle classes, rapid digital adoption and large infrastructure programmes create demand across technology, industry, consumer goods and services. For many European companies, the next phase of growth lies here.

02

Resilience through diversification

Relying on one region exposes a business to a single economic cycle. A second base in Asia spreads revenue, customers and suppliers across markets and time zones, making the whole company stronger.

03

Where innovation and supply chains meet

Asia is home to leading manufacturing ecosystems, fast-moving digital markets and a deep pool of engineering talent. Being present means seeing trends early, partnering with the right players and adapting products faster.

Closer to the market

Asia is not won from a distance. It is won on the ground.

Why Singapore

The natural gateway for European business in Asia.

Singapore combines European-style predictability with a front-row seat to Asia's growth. It is where many international companies choose to place their regional headquarters, and it is where LPSP Asia is based.

Rule of law and IP protection

A transparent legal system based on English common law, strong protection of intellectual property and a reputation for low corruption.

English as the language of business

Contracts, government services and daily business run in English, with a multilingual workforce that also speaks Chinese, Malay and Tamil.

Competitive and clear taxation

A headline corporate tax rate of 17%, a wide network of double-tax agreements and a straightforward system for setting up and running a company.

A hub for the whole region

One of the world's best-connected airports and ports puts the major markets of Southeast Asia, China, India and Australia within easy reach.

Access through trade agreements

The EU–Singapore Free Trade Agreement and Singapore's broad network of regional agreements, including RCEP, open doors across Asia-Pacific.

Capital, talent and innovation

A leading financial centre with active investors, strong universities and a government that actively supports technology, AI and innovation.

FAQ

Questions European companies ask us first.

Why use Singapore as a base for Asia?

Singapore is a regional headquarters location for many international companies. It offers a stable legal and regulatory environment, English as the language of business, and strong connections to markets across Southeast Asia and the wider region.

Does a Singapore company need a local director?

Yes. A Singapore company must have at least one director who is ordinarily resident in Singapore. We explain the options and help you choose the right structure during the planning phase.

How long does market entry take?

Registering a company can be quick once documents are ready. Building a working local presence, with partners, customers and people on the ground, usually takes several months. Our Landing Programme is designed around 12 weeks.

Do you only work with technology companies?

No. We work with European companies across sectors. Technology and AI is an area of particular depth for our directors.

Can you help our executives relocate?

Yes. Our Executive Landing service gives practical support to entrepreneurs and executives moving to Singapore and Asia, so they can focus on the business from day one.

Can we work with you in German?

Yes. We work with clients in English and German.
